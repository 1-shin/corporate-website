Privacy Policy

Shin Inc. (“we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through this website (https://1-shin.com/) as described below.

1. Information we collect

Through our contact form we collect your company name, your name, your email address and the content of your enquiry.

In addition, access date and time, IP address and browser type are automatically recorded in the access logs of our hosting provider.

2. How we use it

We use the information to reply to your enquiry, to communicate with you about business and proposals, and to improve the quality of our services. We do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Disclosure to third parties

We do not disclose personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law. However, we may share information with partner factories, logistics providers and other subcontractors to the extent necessary to plan, produce and ship the items you have asked us to make. In such cases we supervise those subcontractors appropriately.

4. Third-party services

This website uses the services listed below. Information may be sent to each provider and is then handled under that provider's own privacy policy.

  • Netlify (hosting and form delivery)
    Serves this website and receives the contents of the contact form. Privacy policy
  • Google Maps (map display)
    A map is embedded in the company profile. Your IP address and similar data are sent to Google when the page loads. Privacy policy

5. Cookies

We do not set cookies to identify you. The third-party services above may set their own cookies. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings.

6. Access, correction and deletion

If you ask us to disclose, correct, suspend the use of, or delete the personal information we hold about you, we will verify your identity and respond promptly in accordance with applicable law. Please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy in response to changes in law or in our business. Any revised version applies from the moment it is published on this page.
