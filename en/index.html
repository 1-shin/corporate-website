ABOUT US

Shin Inc. is based in Shibuya, Tokyo, the heart of the fashion industry.

We support the production of fashion items which are made in Japan, overseeing the entire process.

Japanese clients demand the highest quality, so factories always adhere to the strict standards. We are proud of our “Made in Japan” items and focus on providing each brand with what they really want at a high quality and fair price.

Japan has a wide variety of clothing materials. Japan has maintained traditional techniques for making original fabrics since ancient times, as well as cutting-edge techniques for making sustainable and futuristic materials.

Depending on your needs, we can arrange materials, factories, an inspection centre, shipping, and so on, based on your designs and requests. If you want to manufacture in Japan, we offer a one-stop service.

PROCESS

From first consultation to delivery

MATERIAL

Materials

For both apparel and footwear, we source materials that match your request, whatever the country of production. Original fabrics, leathers and yarns can also be developed.

We can also help with hang tags, brand labels, care labels, packaging and more.

DESIGN

Design

Design proposals and pattern making are also available.

Leave embroidery, printing and other finishing, original parts, shoe outsoles and one-piece moulded EVA sandals to us — from design to development.

FACTORY

Factories

Based on your concept, we decide the best production setup together — considering quantity, price and the feel you want.

We can also propose cross-factory combinations, such as knit-and-woven or belt-and-bag pairings.

LEAD TIME

Lead time

Roughly 1–3 months to the confirmed sample, then 2–3 months from production to inspection and delivery.

Longer around sample revisions and factory holidays (summer break, Lunar New Year).

LOT

Minimum order

Minimum lots vary by item, specification and country of production.

Small lots are possible depending on the project — just ask.
